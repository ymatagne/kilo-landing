The manifesto

Six nos. Not negotiable.

  1. No calories.

    Not shown, not guessed, not stored. The sticker is a love score, not a measurement.

  2. No stars.

    The only review is Worth it. Oui or Non.

  3. No strangers.

    Your crew, up to six. No explore tab, no public profiles.

  4. No lighter option.

    No health tags, no portion nudges. We don't editorialize.

  5. No likes.

    One reaction: Jealous. It's the honest one.

  6. No shame.

    Every joke lands on the plate. Never the person.
